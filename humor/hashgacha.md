Some might be concerned that this brilliant idea won't work. Some Jews, the lax ones, decide all on their own who to trust and where they are willing to eat. But remember, this has already been successfully done! Two hundred years ago, there was no Ⓤ or floral Haredi symbols. Jews knew which businesses they trusted in their communities. Starting with butchers and focusing on large cities, official Hashgocho gradually became a must.

Here's how we will succeed: There's already a strong demand from caterers and bakers who use a home kitchen but want to sell to kosher consumers. 

Next, move on to synagogues. They'll demand certification for anyone who brings kiddush or Bar-Mitzvah food--after all, we don't want ham sandwiches in the shul kitchen, right?! Hashgocho is fair and neutral, bypassing the favoritism of today's personal trust. 

Soon, we expand to the whole community. No requirement, feel free not to have one, but how do you want your home to be known? 

Before you know it, it's as much of an expectation for families as for restaurants.
